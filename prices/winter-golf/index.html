How to dress for winter golf

Three layers, not one big coat

A thin base layer to keep sweat off your skin, a warm mid-layer, and a waterproof or windproof shell on top. Big coats restrict your turn; layers don’t, and you can shed one as you warm up.

Waterproof or water-resistant?

“Water-resistant” copes with a shower. For proper rain you want fully waterproof, with taped seams. Waterproof ratings are given in millimetres: around 10,000mm and up is solid for golf. Some brands also guarantee their waterproofs for a year or more; the retailer page will say.

What to buy first

Hands and feet first: winter gloves or mitts between shots, and waterproof shoes. Then waterproof trousers, which get wetter than jackets on long grass. A hat costs little and makes a big difference.
