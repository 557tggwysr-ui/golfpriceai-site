Questions

When is Black Friday 2026 in the UK?

Friday 27 November 2026. Cyber Monday is Monday 30 November. Many golf retailers start their offers a week or two earlier.

How do you know a Black Friday golf deal is real?

We compare the Black Friday price with the model’s October price: the typical lowest new, in-stock price we recorded in October, checking every 6 hours. A retailer’s own “was” or RRP price is not used.

What counts as cheaper on this page?

At least £5 and 5% below the October price. Smaller wobbles happen every week and aren’t worth a special trip.

What does “Lowest we’ve tracked” mean?

Today’s price is the lowest we’ve recorded for that model since we started tracking it. Each model’s page shows its full price history so you can see for yourself.
