Common questions

How much should I pay for golf shoes?

Today a typical men’s pair costs about £99.97, and the cheapest men’s golf shoes start from £29.99. Anything marked “Lowest we’ve tracked” is cheaper than we’ve seen it before.

When are golf shoes cheapest?

Prices tend to move when new ranges arrive and older colours are cleared, and at sale times like Black Friday. The drops table above shows which ones are genuinely cheaper right now, against our own price history.

Are these new?

Yes. These guides only cover new stock that's in stock now. Junior sizes and second-hand items are left out.

Are the price drops real?

Yes. A drop only counts when today's price is the lowest we've recorded for it and at least £5 and 3% below its highest tracked price. We never use a retailer's own “was” price.
