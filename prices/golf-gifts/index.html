Questions

What does “Gift-safe” mean?

You don’t need to know their sizes, which hand they play or their club specs: golf balls, rangefinders, GPS watches, towels, umbrellas, trolleys and practice kit. Gloves, clothing and clubs say “Check first” and what to find out.

How does “Drop a hint” work?

Save the gifts you’d like with the heart, then tap “Send it as a hint”. It opens WhatsApp or your phone’s share menu with a link to your list, showing today’s prices. We never email anyone on your behalf.

Are the prices up to date?

Yes. We check every listing at the UK retailers we track every 6 hours. Prices can change on the retailer’s site, so check there before you buy.
