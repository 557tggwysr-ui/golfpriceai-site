Buying a junior set as a gift?

Size matters most

Clubs that are too long are harder to swing. If a child is between sizes, most coaches suggest the size that fits now rather than one to grow into.

How many clubs?

Young beginners only need 3 to 5 clubs. Bigger sets suit children who already play or have lessons.

Watch the price

Open any set’s price page and tap “Email me when it drops”. We’ll tell you if it gets cheaper before you buy.

Common questions

What size junior golf set do I need?

Go by height rather than age. Measure your child in their shoes and pick the set made for that height; sets sold by age give a rough range, and the retailer’s size chart is the final word.

How much does a junior golf set cost?

Today junior sets start from £99.00, and a typical set is about £209.00. Prices rise with the number of clubs and the size of the set.

Are these prices for new sets?

Yes. We only list new stock that’s in stock now, and check every price every 6 hours across multiple UK retailers.

Are the price drops real?

Yes. A set is marked “Lowest we’ve tracked” only when today’s price is the lowest we’ve recorded for it, checked against our own price history.
