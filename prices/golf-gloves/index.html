Common questions

How much should I pay for golf gloves?

Today a typical men’s glove costs about £17.99, and the cheapest men’s golf gloves start from £6.95. Anything marked “Lowest we’ve tracked” is cheaper than we’ve seen it before.

How often should I replace my golf glove?

It depends how often you play, but a glove that's shiny, stretched or torn won't grip properly. Multi-packs and genuine drops are the cheapest way to keep a fresh one in the bag.

Are these new?

Yes. These guides only cover new stock that's in stock now. Junior sizes and second-hand items are left out.

Are the price drops real?

Yes. A drop only counts when today's price is the lowest we've recorded for it and at least £5 and 3% below its highest tracked price. We never use a retailer's own “was” price.
