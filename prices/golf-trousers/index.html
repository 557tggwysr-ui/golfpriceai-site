Common questions

How much should I pay for golf trousers?

Today a typical men’s pair costs about £54.95, and the cheapest men’s golf trousers start from £21.25. Anything marked “Lowest we’ve tracked” is cheaper than we’ve seen it before.

Do I need waterproof golf trousers?

If you play through the winter, a pair is worth having. Waterproof trousers go over normal golf trousers, so you only need them when it rains.

Are these new?

Yes. These guides only cover new stock that's in stock now. Junior sizes and second-hand items are left out.

Are the price drops real?
