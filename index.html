    Common questions

    What is GolfPrice AI?

    A UK golf price comparison site. We compare prices on golf clubs, clothing, shoes and accessories across multiple UK retailers every 6 hours, keep our own price history for each product, and tell you when a price is genuinely good.

    How do I find the cheapest price for a golf club?

    Search for it above, or open its price guide. Each guide shows today's lowest new UK price, which retailer has it, and a verdict on whether to buy now or wait. Seen a price elsewhere? Check if it's any good.

    How often are prices checked?

    Every 6 hours. Each check pulls the latest prices and stock from the retailers' own product feeds, so what you see is what they're charging now.

    Are the discounts real?

    We only call something a price drop when it's cheaper than the prices we've recorded ourselves, by at least £5 and 3%. We don't trust a retailer's own “was” price, and there are no countdown timers or fake “only 2 left” messages.

    Does it cost more to buy through GolfPrice AI?

    No. You pay the retailer's normal price. We may earn a small commission from some retailers, and it never changes the price you pay or the order things are listed in.
