Questions golfers in Glasgow and Clyde ask

How many golf courses are there in Glasgow and Clyde?

There are 128 golf courses in Glasgow and Clyde on our UK course list (from OpenStreetMap), with the most around Glasgow (17), Clarkston (4), East Kilbride (4). Every one has its own page with the courses nearby and its ace board.

How do I join a golf society in Glasgow and Clyde?

No Glasgow and Clyde golf societies are listed yet. Open a society’s page, tap “Ask to join”, and the organiser answers you there. Some also list spare places on their next golf day, so you can try a day before you join.

Do I need a handicap to join a golf society?

Usually not. Most golf societies welcome golfers without an official handicap and give you one after a few rounds; some ask for an official handicap. Each society’s listing says which, and whether beginners are welcome.

How much does a golf society day cost?

Society green fees are typically about £30 to £55 a golfer, or roughly £67 to £75 with a bacon roll and a two-course meal, from published club society rates. Our guide to golf society day costs has a free calculator.

Is there a golf society league in Glasgow and Clyde?

Yes. Glasgow and Clyde golf societies can enter the free National Society League in the Scotland region for the 2027 season (April to October), scored from their society days, with promotion and relegation.
