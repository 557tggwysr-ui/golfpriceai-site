Questions golfers in Derbyshire ask

How many golf courses are there in Derbyshire?

There are 41 golf courses in Derbyshire on our UK course list (from OpenStreetMap), with the most around Derby (7), Chesterfield (6), Ilkeston (3). Every one has its own page with the courses nearby and its ace board.

How do I join a golf society in Derbyshire?

No Derbyshire golf societies are listed yet. Open a society’s page, tap “Ask to join”, and the organiser answers you there. Some also list spare places on their next golf day, so you can try a day before you join.

Do I need a handicap to join a golf society?

Usually not. Most golf societies welcome golfers without an official handicap and give you one after a few rounds; some ask for an official handicap. Each society’s listing says which, and whether beginners are welcome.

How much does a golf society day cost?

Society green fees are typically about £30 to £55 a golfer, or roughly £67 to £75 with a bacon roll and a two-course meal, from published club society rates. Our guide to golf society day costs has a free calculator.

Is there a golf society league in Derbyshire?

Yes. Derbyshire golf societies can enter the free National Society League in the Midlands region for the 2027 season (April to October), scored from their society days, with promotion and relegation.
