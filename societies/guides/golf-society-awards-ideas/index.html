Golf society awards ideas: 40 awards for your end-of-season night

The best golf society awards mix a few proper trophies (Player of the Year, the Order of Merit, Most Improved) with funny awards everyone remembers (Bandit of the Year, Ball Donor of the Year) and a wooden spoon. Most golf societies give 6 to 10 awards, with £10 to £50 of prizes each.

Running the golf society awards night

  1. Lock the numbers first. Settle the Order of Merit and Player of the Year from the season’s results before anyone has a drink.
  2. Poll the funny awards. A WhatsApp poll a week before keeps it fair and builds the suspense.
  3. Funny first, trophies last. Start with the booby prizes, build through the funny awards and finish on Player of the Year.
  4. One line per winner. A short, kind roast beats a long speech. Each card above has one ready.
  5. Put it on the big screen. The kit’s slideshow runs from a phone or laptop, one award at a time.
  6. Share the cards after. Drop them in the group chat so everyone has the proof.

Golf society awards: questions organisers ask

How many awards should a golf society give?

Most golf societies give between 6 and 10 at the end-of-season night: two or three proper trophies (Player of the Year, the Order of Merit, Most Improved), four to six funny awards, and a wooden spoon. More than about 12 and the presentation drags.

Who decides the funny golf society awards?

Usually the organiser or the committee, but a vote in the WhatsApp group works brilliantly: put up a poll a week before the night and announce the winners on the night. Keep the proper trophies on the numbers, so nobody can argue.

What prizes do golf societies give?

A trophy or engraved glass for Player of the Year, then golf kit for the rest: a dozen balls, a glove, a towel or a headcover. Many societies set a budget per award and let the winner choose. The Awards Night Kit’s Prize Picker does exactly that, at today’s lowest UK prices.

How much should a golf society spend on end-of-season prizes?

A common pattern is £40 to £75 for Player of the Year, £20 to £35 for the other proper trophies and £10 to £20 for each funny award, paid from a season prize fund or a few pounds added to each society day’s entry.
