Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

GolfPrice AI ("we", "us", "our") operates golfpriceai.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We've written it in plain language rather than dense legal text.

What we collect

  • Email address — only if you sign up for deal alerts or ask us to email you your bag from Assemble Your Arsenal. We use it to send you the emails you asked for, and nothing else.
  • The products you're watching — if you ask us to email you when a particular club, ball or other product drops in price or comes back in stock, we store which ones, plus the date and time you confirmed your sign-up.
  • The price you paid — only if you use “Bought it? We’ll keep watching” on a price page. We store the product, the price you paid and the date, so we can email you once if the price drops within 30 days. It’s deleted after that email, or after 30 days, whichever comes first.
  • Fourball Alerts — when you send a price page or shortlist to your four-ball, the link goes through your own WhatsApp or share menu: we never see or store who you send it to. The link carries an optional first name you type and a random invite code. We count how many people sign up through each invite code (a number only, with no names or email addresses attached) so the page can show “3 golfers joined”. Your shortlist is kept in your own browser, not by us.
  • Demand Index counts — for our UK Golf Demand Index we keep anonymous daily totals: how many times each price page was viewed, how many times a model was added to a shortlist, the words typed into our search, and, when you finish a round of Par Price, how far each guess was from the real price (a ratio, not your score). No cookie is set and nothing is stored in your browser for this. Your IP address is never stored: it is turned into a one-way scrambled code that changes every day and can’t be turned back into an address, kept for no more than two days so each visitor counts once per product per day, and never saved with the counts. The totals are deleted after 70 days, and we publish only rankings and percentages, never raw search words that don’t match a golf brand or product. We also count how many people watch each product through deal alerts (a number per product, no email addresses).
  • Usage data — standard, non-identifying information like which pages you visit and which browser you're using, collected automatically to help us understand what's useful and fix what isn't.
  • Cookies — small files stored in your browser. See our Cookie Policy for the full breakdown of what we use and why.

How we use it

  • To send deal alert, back-in-stock and “Bought it?” price-drop emails to people who've signed up for them
  • To send a one-off email of your bag when you ask for one
  • To understand which pages and deals people find useful, so we can improve the site
  • To keep the site secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Affiliate links

GolfPrice AI is reader-supported. When you click a product link on this site and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. This doesn't affect the price you pay, and it doesn't influence which deals we choose to feature — see our affiliate disclosure for more detail.

Deal alerts: how sign-up works and who handles your email

  • Confirm first. When you enter your email we send one message asking you to confirm. Until you click the button in it, you're not signed up and we send nothing else. If you don't click it, the link simply expires after 7 days.
  • What we keep: your email address, the models you're watching, and when you confirmed. Nothing else.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, based in France) stores these details and sends our emails on our behalf. Your data is held in the EU.
  • Cloudflare runs the small service that receives the sign-up form and passes it to Brevo, so our Brevo account key never sits in the website's code. It briefly notes the sign-up's internet address (for up to 10 minutes) to block spam sign-ups, and doesn't keep it.
  • Unsubscribe in one click. Every email has a link to stop alerts for one model or stop all emails. It works straight away, with nothing to log into.

We never add you to anyone else's list, and we never put subscriber details in our website's public code or data files.

"Email me this list" (Assemble Your Arsenal)

When you ask us to email you your bag, we send one email to the address you give, through Brevo, and that's it: you're not added to any list unless you also tick the box for price alerts and then confirm. Brevo keeps a standard record of emails it has sent, as all email services do. To stop the button being misused, our Cloudflare service keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of the address for up to 24 hours, so it can limit how many lists go to one address in a day, and then forgets it.

Your rights

If you're in the UK or EU, you have rights under GDPR including the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email us at the address below.

Data retention

We keep your email address for as long as you're subscribed to deal alerts. If you unsubscribe, you're taken off our mailing list and your watched models are cleared straight away. To have your address deleted from Brevo entirely, email us at the address below and we'll do it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
