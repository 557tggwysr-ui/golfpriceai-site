Ask ChatGPT or Claude for live UK golf prices

Add GolfPrice AI to your AI assistant and it can check today’s lowest UK price for a club, ball or gadget, its price history, and this week’s genuine price drops, then link you to the page with every retailer. Prices are checked every 6 hours across multiple UK retailers.

The address to add:

https://golfpriceai-mcp.golfpriceai.workers.dev/mcp

No account, no sign-in. It only reads public prices from golfpriceai.com, and can’t see your chats, files or anything else.

In Claude

  1. Open Settings, then Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it GolfPrice AI and paste the address above.
  4. Click Add. Done.

Works on every Claude plan, including free (which allows one custom connector). On Team and Enterprise plans an owner adds it first.

In ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings, then Apps & Connectors.
  2. At the bottom, open Advanced settings and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  4. Name it GolfPrice AI, paste the address above, set authentication to None, and click Create.

Needs ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu. Menu names change now and then; if yours look different, search ChatGPT’s help for “developer mode connectors”.

Good to know

What does it cover?

Every model with a GolfPrice AI price page: drivers, woods, hybrids, iron sets, wedges, putters, balls, bags, GPS watches, rangefinders, electric trolleys and launch monitors. New stock only. Plus golf society help: prize ideas at live prices, the prize fund split, Find a Society and our golf society guides, and World Handicap System handicap sums.

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free. We may earn a commission if you buy through our links; it never changes the price or what the assistant is told.

Can it buy things or see my data?

No. It can only look up public prices. It has no access to your chats, files, email or accounts, and we store nothing about you.

The same answers are on the site: the price checker, price drops today and every price guide.
