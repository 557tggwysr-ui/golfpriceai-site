Is Black Friday golf genuinely cheaper? What our prices show

By John Willson, Founder, GolfPrice AI · Data to 19 November 2026

Short answer: some of it. Of 1,207 golf models we've tracked since mid-October, 346 (29%) were genuinely cheaper a week before Black Friday than they were in mid-October, 11 cost more, and the rest hadn't moved. Drivers were the most likely to have dropped (54%), followed by GPS watches and fairway woods; golf bags and electric trolleys the least.

Every November the same question comes up in golf group chats: is the Black Friday price a real saving, or the normal price with a red sticker on it? We track the prices of golf kit across multiple UK retailers every six hours, so instead of guessing, we checked. We compared each model's lowest price on 15 October 2026 with its lowest price on 19 November 2026, a week before Black Friday.

Each of these is the lowest price we found for that model across the retailers we track, on that date. Prices change all the time, so check the live page before you buy.

What about prices going up before the sales?

A common worry is that prices are nudged up in early November so the Black Friday "saving" looks bigger. In our data that was rare: we found 2 models whose price rose after mid-October and then came back down by 19 November 2026. Most prices either fell or stayed put. That's one reason we compare against a fixed earlier date rather than a "was" price.

How to check a deal yourself

Compare against what the same model cost a few weeks ago, not against the price on the sticker. Every price page on GolfPrice AI shows the price history and a plain verdict, and our Black Friday page lists only the models that are genuinely cheaper than in October, updated every six hours.
