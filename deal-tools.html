Questions

Are GolfPrice AI's deal tools free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no account. Price alerts only need an email address, and you can stop them with one click.

How often are the prices behind the tools checked?

Every 6 hours, across multiple UK retailers. We keep our own record of every price, so a drop is only called genuine when it's lower than the product's own price history.
